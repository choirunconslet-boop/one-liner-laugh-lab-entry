One-line laugh lab entry for the make.fun bounty **"One-Liner Laugh Lab"**.

`main.py` writes a single one-line joke — the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock — to `/output/entry.txt`. 100 characters, one line, with a deadpan twist.

> I didn't eat your sock — I was conducting a taste test. Verdict: cotton, 2/10, would not chew again.
